# FAQ: What Is This Email and What Do I Do?

Short answers to the questions people ask most, plus where each piece of information comes from. If an email sent you here, find its title below. New to the terms? See <a href="guide.html?role=hr&amp;file=guides%2Fdefinitions.md">Definitions</a>.

<div class="callout"><strong>TLDR:</strong> HR starts a case, the system builds the checklist and sends the requests, and you only hear from it when something needs <em>you</em>. Every email says what to do and links to the page where you do it.</div>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>"Missing details": a case can't move forward</summary> <div class="accordion-body">


**Who gets it:** the **Case Owner** (usually the person who submitted the form).

<div class="numbered-group">
<strong>What it means:</strong> a request needs an employee's name, effective date, location, etc. and one of them is blank on the case. Every request on that case is on hold until it's filled in. You get <strong>one</strong> email per case, not one per request.</div>

**What to do:**
1. Click **Open Case Progress** in the email.
2. Open the case and fill in what the email lists under **Missing**.
3. That's it. Everything that was waiting goes out on its own, and nobody needs to resubmit anything.

**Not your case anymore?** 
Change **Case Owner** on the case to the right person. Future notices go to them.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>"Waiting on a step": a request is held up by another step</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

**Who gets it:** the manager.

<div class="numbered-group">
<strong>What it means:</strong> some requests can only go out after another step is done. For example, the Local 393 dispatch request waits until the dispatch form has been submitted. The email names the step to finish.</div>

**What to do:** click **Open Manager Triage**, find the step it names and tick **Mark Complete** once it's done. The waiting request then sends, or drafts for your review, on its own.

<div class="callout"><strong>Only Local 393 has this dispatch step.</strong> Every other local skips it, and their dispatch request goes out on the normal schedule.</div>

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>"Draft ready for review"</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

**Who gets it:** managers set to **Review &amp; Send**, and all users for sensitive material requests (these are always manually reviewed and approved).

<div class="numbered-group">
<strong>What to do:</strong> open the <strong>Draft Queue</strong>, check the draft, and either click <strong>Open in Outlook</strong>, send it and tick <strong>Sent from Outlook</strong>, or tick <strong>Send For Me</strong>.</div> 
  
See <a href="guide.html?role=manager&amp;file=guides%2Fmanager-sending-requests.md">Sending Requests</a>.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>"New step ready": why don't I get this for every step?</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

You only get it when the new step is something **you do by hand**, like training or collecting equipment.

When the new step is an email request, you don't get a separate "new step" notice. The request itself is the notice: either it sends, or it lands in your Draft Queue with a "draft ready" email. One action, one email.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>A step says "Follow Up". What's left to do?</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

Some requests aren't finished when the email goes out. A laptop still has to arrive; a vehicle policy still needs the employee's signature. Those steps move to **Follow Up** instead of Complete, and the **Follow-Up Instructions** on the step say exactly what's left.

<div class="numbered-group">
Do what it says, then tick **Mark Complete**.</div>

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Role changes: who does what?</summary> <div class="accordion-body">
  
<ol>
<li><strong>The manager files</strong> the Role Change form. Only fill in what's changing; blank fields keep the employee's current details.</li>
<li><strong>HR gets a "Role change to review" email</strong> listing every change.</li>
<li><strong>HR checks it and ticks HR Approved</strong> on the case. Until then nothing happens: no steps, no requests, no record changes.</li>
<li>After approval, the steps are created (payroll, IT access, union transfer and so on, only for what's changing). On the effective date, the employee's record and crew record update themselves.</li>
</ol>

<div class="numbered-group">
<strong>Is a level change a role change?</strong> Yes. A change of level, union local or team usually changes pay, dues or the labor agreement, so it goes through the Role Change form and HR approval. A service manager doesn't edit those directly.</div>

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Why am I getting so many emails? Can I turn some off?</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

Managers can turn off most notices on <a href="guide.html?role=manager&amp;file=guides%2Fmanager-email-settings.md">Email Settings</a>. The steps still happen and still show on your pages. Two notices always stay on: the settings-changed confirmation and HR check-ins.

The system is built to send one email per thing you need to do:
- missing details are reported once per case, not once per request
- a new email step doesn't also send a "new step" notice
- the Monday roll-up groups all due tasks into one email

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Something looks wrong. Who do I ask?</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

Reply to the email. It goes back to the case manager or HR. For anything about the employee's details, contact the **Case Owner**. 

For how the system works, see <a href="guide.html?role=hr&amp;file=guides%2Fhow-it-works.md">How It Works</a>.

</div> </details>
