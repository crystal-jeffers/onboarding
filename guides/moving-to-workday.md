# Moving to Workday

Southland moves HR onto **Workday in late November 2026**. This system was built as the bridge until then. It turns the superintendents' New Hire Protocols into one tracked checklist, and it marks which steps Workday is expected to take over. That way, the hand-off is planned instead of improvised.

<div class="callout"><strong>These are estimates.</strong> Each step's Workday tag is a best guess based on how Workday usually handles hiring, job changes, and terminations. HR will confirm the real list once Southland's Workday setup is final.</div>

## What changes

Workday runs HR's part of each case as a guided workflow, so the "send an email to start it" steps go away. The new hire, HR, and the manager each get their tasks in Workday instead.

| Moves to Workday | Probably moves | Depends on setup |
|---|---|---|
| New hire paperwork and tax forms | Background check and drug screen | Phone, iPad, and hotspot |
| Direct deposit | E-Verify | Bluebeam and other software |
| I-9 Section 1 | Final paycheck | Key fobs and badge access |
| HR record and job title | Laptop and system access | Welcome email |
| Payroll updates | California forms and notices | PCard |
| Org chart and distribution lists | Exit interview | Equipment collection |
| Emergency contacts | 30/60/90-day check-ins | Mailbox forwarding |
| COBRA notice | | |

## What stays

About half the checklist is specific to Start-Up, so Workday won't know it exists. These steps stay here, with the manager:

- Union dispatch, Local 393 forms, and union hall notices
- The vehicle policy, MVR, Fleet, DOT card, and vehicle return
- The Home Depot card
- Vehicle tools, arc flash gear, and Tools Issued
- Safety onboarding and NFPA 70E training
- Day 1, Day 3, and first-week field training
- Key contacts, business cards, and the dispatch calendar

## How the hand-off works

1. **Before go-live.** Everything runs here, as it does today.
2. **At go-live.** HR confirms each step's tag. Steps confirmed as **Workday** are switched off for new cases. Cases already in progress finish here, so nothing is dropped midway.
3. **After go-live.** The checklist keeps the department-specific steps and points to Workday for the rest. Managers still see the whole process and who owns each step, so they know who to follow up with.

## Where to see the tags

- **HR:** in the Task Library, the **Workday Impact** column shows each step's tag, and **Workday Notes** says what a "probably" or "depends" tag is waiting on.
- **Managers:** the [New Hire Checklist](guide.html?role=manager&amp;file=guides%2Fmanager-checklist.md) marks the steps that may move to Workday.
