# Keeping Cases Moving

The system sends the routine requests. HR's job is the exceptions: steps that stall, owners who haven't responded, and data that's missing.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Your daily HR email (7:15 AM)</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<figure class="shot thumb">
  <img src="assets/img/emails/email-hr-open-items.png" alt="Daily HR open items email with Third Party and Employee tables" loading="lazy">
  <figcaption>Daily HR email. Tap to enlarge.</figcaption>
</figure>

- Two tables: steps waiting on **third parties** (background check, union hall, safety scheduling) and steps waiting on **the employee** (portal, I-9, direct deposit).
- **Current phase only.** A hire starting in six weeks doesn't fill your inbox with their Day 1 tasks. Each step appears once its phase begins, and stays until it's done.
- No email on days with nothing open.

<div class="clear"></div>

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Nudging a manager</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<ol class="chunked-list">
<li>Open <strong>Manager Settings</strong> in the HR Workspace.</li>
<li>Tick <strong>Remind Manager</strong> on their row.</li>
<li>They get one email listing everything overdue or due soon across all their cases, with a <strong>Responsible</strong> column so they know who to chase.</li>
<li>The box clears itself, and <strong>Last Reminded</strong> shows when you last nudged them.</li>
</ol>

Managers who leave the **Monday roll-up** on get the same list every week without a nudge.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>When a request can't send</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

A request waits (and the manager is told why) when:

- The case is missing the employee's name, date, or location, **or**
- The step's prerequisite isn't done yet.

The task's **Action Needed** field says exactly what to fix. Once it's fixed, the request sends by itself. Nobody needs to resubmit anything.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Closing the loop</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

- When a request is done, its completion step appears (badge deactivated, payroll updated). If **Confirmed By** is HR, it shows on your daily Open Items email. Tick **Mark Complete** once the team confirms.
- A case closes itself when every step is complete. Onboardings, role changes, and offboardings each get their own closing note (no "Congrats!" on a departure).

</div> </details>
