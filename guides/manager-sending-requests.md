# Sending Requests

Most of what a new hire needs (a laptop, a phone, building access, union dispatch, a vehicle policy) starts with an email from you. The system writes those emails, fills in the person's details and sends them on time. How much you're involved is up to you.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Pick your send mode</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<div class="table-scroll">
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Mode</th><th>What happens when a request is due</th><th>Good for</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><strong>Fully Automated</strong></td><td>It sends in your name, with replies coming back to you. You don't have to do anything.</td><td>Managers who trust the templates and want zero admin.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Review &amp; Send</strong></td><td>It's drafted into your <strong>Draft Queue</strong>, and you check it and send it.</td><td>New managers, or anyone who likes to add a personal line.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

Change it anytime on <strong>Email Settings</strong>. Switching takes effect immediately: requests already due either send (Fully Automated) or drop into your Draft Queue (Review &amp; Send).

<div class="callout"><strong>Always reviewed, whatever your mode:</strong> emails to the union hall, and every email on an involuntary departure. They always land in your Draft Queue first.</div>

</div> </details>

<div class="numbered-group">
<h4>Option 1: send it from Outlook</h4>
<ol>
<li>Click <strong>Open in Outlook</strong>. A new email opens with To, CC, subject and body filled in.</li>
<li>Edit it if you like, then send it from Outlook as normal.</li>
<li>Back in Airtable, tick <strong>Sent from Outlook</strong>. The step is logged as sent and either completes or moves to Follow Up.</li>
</ol>
</div>

<div class="numbered-group">
<h4>Option 2: let Airtable send it</h4>
<ol>
<li>Tick <strong>Send Now</strong>.</li>
<li>It sends within seconds in your name, with replies coming back to you, and is logged automatically.</li>
</ol>
</div>

- Opening Outlook doesn't log anything by itself. Tick **Sent from Outlook** once you've sent it, or it'll keep showing as open.
- Very long emails can be cut short by some mail apps. The current templates are well under that limit.

</div> </details>

<ol class="chunked-list">
<li>Open a draft. <strong>Send To</strong>, <strong>CC</strong>, <strong>Subject</strong> and <strong>Body</strong> are all editable, and your changes only affect this one email.</li>
<li>Then either click <strong>Open in Outlook</strong>, send it and tick <strong>Sent from Outlook</strong>, <em>or</em> tick <strong>Send For Me</strong> to have Airtable send it.</li>
<li>It can't send twice, even if you tick both boxes.</li>
</ol>

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>After it's sent</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

- Requests that need a next step (laptop delivered, fob handed over, policy signed) move to **Follow Up**. The step's **Follow-Up Instructions** say exactly what's left. Tick **Mark Complete** once it's done.
- Everything else completes as soon as it's sent.
- Some steps unlock the next one. For example, submitting the Local 393 dispatch form unlocks the dispatch request, and you'll get a notice when it appears.

</div> </details>
