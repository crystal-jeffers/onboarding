# Sending Requests

Most of what a new hire needs (a laptop, a phone, building access, or a vehicle) starts with an email from you. The system writes those emails, fills in the person's details, and sends them on time. How much you're involved is up to you.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Pick your send mode</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<div class="table-scroll">
<table>
<thead><tr><th>Mode</th><th>When a request is due</th><th>Good for</th></tr></thead>
<tbody>
<tr><td><strong>Fully Automated</strong></td><td>Emails send automatically. (in your name, with replies coming back to you. You don't have to do anything.)</td><td>Managers who trust the templates and want zero admin.</td></tr>
<tr><td><strong>Review &amp; Send</strong></td><td>Emails are automatically drafted into your <strong>Draft Queue</strong>. You check it and send it.</td><td>New managers, special notes, or anyone who likes to add a personal line.</td></tr>
</tbody>
</table>
</div>

Change it anytime on <strong>Email Settings</strong>. Switching takes effect immediately: requests already due either send (Fully Automated) or drop into your Draft Queue (Review &amp; Send).

<div class="callout"><strong>Always reviewed, whatever your mode:</strong> emails to the union hall, and every email on an involuntary departure. They always land in your Draft Queue first.</div>

</div> </details>

<div class="numbered-group">
<h4>Option 1: Send it from Outlook</h4>
<ol>
<li>Click <strong>Open in Outlook</strong>.</li>
<li>A new email opens with To, CC, subject, and body filled in.</li>
<li>Edit it if you like, then send it from Outlook as normal.</li>
<li>Back in Airtable, <strong>Mark Complete</strong>. If someone else has to deliver it, the completion step appears next.</li>
</ol>
</div>

<div class="numbered-group">
<h4>Option 2: Send it from Airtable</h4>
<ol>
<li>Tick <strong>Send Now</strong>.</li>
<li>It sends within seconds (in your name, with replies coming back to you) and is marked Complete automatically.</li>
</ol>
</div>

- Opening Outlook doesn't log anything by itself. **Mark Complete** once you've sent it, or it'll keep showing as open.
- Very long emails can be cut short by some mail apps. The current templates are well under that limit.

<details class="accordion"> <summary>After it's sent</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<div class="numbered-group">
A request is done once it's sent. If someone else has to deliver (laptop ready, fob issued, policy signed), a <strong>completion step</strong> appears on your Triage with what to check. Tick it once they confirm.</div>
<div class="numbered-group">
Some steps unlock the next one. For example, submitting the Local 393 dispatch form unlocks the dispatch request, and you'll get a notice when it appears.</div>

</div> </details>
