# Manager Workspace Tour

Your people, what's due for them and what's waiting on you, all in one place. You'll only see your own team's cases.

<div class="callout"><strong>Five minutes a day:</strong> check <strong>Triage</strong> for what's due, clear your <strong>Draft Queue</strong> if you review requests before they send, and use <strong>Quick Deploy</strong> to send anything you want out early.</div>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Dashboard</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<figure class="shot">
  <img src="assets/img/manager/mgr-dashboard.png" alt="Manager Dashboard with active hires, overdue, due soon and progress by stage" loading="lazy">
</figure>

- Your active hires, and how many of your steps are overdue or due in the next 3 days.
- **Hire Progress:** each person's progress through Before Start, Day 1, First Week and Post Start.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Triage: your to-do list</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<figure class="shot">
  <img src="assets/img/manager/mgr-triage.png" alt="Manager Triage list of steps due, grouped by person" loading="lazy">
</figure>

- Every open step on your cases, soonest first. The **Case Type** column tells an onboarding from a role change or a departure.
- **Responsible** shows who needs to act. If it's IT or Safety, a nudge from you helps.
- Tick <strong>Mark Complete</strong> when a hands-on step is done (training held, equipment collected).
- Adding <strong>Progress Notes</strong> to a step moves it to In Progress automatically.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Quick Deploy and Draft Queue</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

These are the two places you send requests from. See <a href="guide.html?role=manager&amp;file=guides%2Fmanager-sending-requests.md">Sending Requests</a> for the full walkthrough.

- **Quick Deploy:** every request that's ready to go now. Open it in Outlook, or send it straight from Airtable.
- **Draft Queue:** requests the system drafted for your review.

</div> </details>

<details class="accordion"> <summary>Overdue</summary> <div class="accordion-body">

<figure class="shot">
  <img src="assets/img/manager/mgr-overdue.png" alt="Overdue steps list" loading="lazy">
</figure>

Everything past due on your cases, with how many days late and what's needed to clear it.

</div> </details>
